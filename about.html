Our Story

How We Started

North Star Bakery opened its doors as a small neighborhood kitchen, started by someone who just wanted to bake good bread for the people around them. What began as early mornings and a single sourdough starter has grown into a daily gathering spot for the block — but the goal hasn't changed: bake by hand, keep it simple, and know the names of the people walking through the door.

Sourcing Philosophy

We buy flour, butter, and produce from local farms and mills whenever we can, and we build our menu around what's actually in season rather than forcing the same thing year-round. Small batches mean less waste and fresher ingredients in everything we bake.
